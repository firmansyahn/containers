NetBox with plugins baked in: the netbox-docker image plus one layer of pinned, hash-checked plugin packages. Derived, not a mirror. Tags are <netbox-docker tag>-r<revision> and are never pushed twice; what each revision holds is in the image's labels and in the revision table of the docs linked here. linux/amd64. Docs: https://github.com/firmansyahn/containers/blob/main/containers/netbox/README.md
