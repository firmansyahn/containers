vip-manager by Cybertec, packaged by Startechnica on minideb trixie (Debian 13) with Bitnami's shell library, rebranded. Runs as uid 1001 and moves a Patroni VIP with only NET_ADMIN, NET_RAW and host networking. Configured through vip-manager's own VIP_* variables or /etc/default/vip-manager.yml. linux/amd64 only; branches 4 and 5 (use 5). Docs: https://github.com/firmansyahn/containers/blob/main/containers/vip-manager/README.md
